# Khazimla2.0

Khazimula Konnect is a digital livestock health and traceability platform built for smallholder and emerging farmers. The platform provides every animal with a lifetime Digital Health Passport, allowing farmers to securely manage health records, vaccinations, treatments, ownership history, and traceability information from birth to sale. For business this platform helps auctioneers make informed purchasing decisions by providing verified health records, vaccination histories, and animal condition assessments. This transparency reduces risk and ensures that buyers pay premium prices only for livestock that has been proven to be healthy and of high value.

By combining affordable technologies such as AI-powered muzzle recognition and Bio-Sensing Patch, Khazimula Konnect helps farmers improve animal health management, prove ownership, increase buyer confidence, and unlock greater livestock value without relying on expensive commercial farming systems.

Key Features
Digital Health Passports
Animal Identification & Traceability
Vaccination & Treatment Tracking
Veterinary Verification
Ownership & Transfer Records
Trusted Livestock History for Buyers and Auctioneers

Our mission is to make livestock management accessible, affordable, and trustworthy for every farmer.
